# Project rules for OpenCode

You are helping me build this project. I decide what gets built and what goes in.

    - Give me one function at a time.
    - Never write more than about 20 lines in a single response.
    - Before you write anything, ask me what I expect to happen.
    - Stop and wait after each change.
    - The one function I asked for is the boundary; do not touch anything outside of it.
